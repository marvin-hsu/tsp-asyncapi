The whole of AsyncAPI 3.1

The document conforms to the AsyncAPI 3.1 specification, supporting channels, operations, messages, schemas, servers and security schemes.

Bindings for many protocols

Kafka, MQTT, AMQP, WebSocket, HTTP, NATS, Pulsar, Google Pub/Sub, Amazon SQS, Anypoint MQ, JMS, IBM MQ and Solace.

Supports Protobuf and Avro payloads

Writes .proto or .avsc definitions from the same source, beside the AsyncAPI document.
